Report, 2026-11-16. Decisions: D47, D48. DESIGN §7.1 is **PROPOSED**, not approved.

- **Built:** `src/lib/diagrams/` (types, geometry rules, hero data, registry), `src/components/diagrams/` (`Diagram`, `HairlineGrid`), two `--animate-*` tokens, `hairline-grid` utility, legend hover (`:has()`), forced-colours rule, `diagrams.hero` messages (it first, en), `/_dev/diagrams` gallery, hero grid layout. No client JavaScript, no dependency, CSP untouched.
- **Defect found while building:** a doc comment with `content/services/*/…` closed the comment at `*/` (parse errors in hero.ts). Reworded.
- **Measured (local, production build, Chromium):** every animation of the figure ends before 4.5 s (designed end 4.3 s; the exact value was not logged); markup passes the 12 KB cap (size not logged); CLS inside the hero is 0 at 1280 and 360; the LCP element is the `h1` at 1280.
- **Measured, surprising:** at 360 px the LCP element is the lead paragraph (`<p>`), not the h1. The diagram is below the CTAs and is never the LCP element; the e2e asserts that, and asserts `H1` only at desktop width.
- **Layout finding, needs your call:** with `items-end` as specified, the primary CTA ends at y 897 at 1280×720 and 1280×800 (below the fold) and fits at 1920×1080 (y 893 of 1080). The header "Parliamone" CTA stays visible. One-class fix if you want it: `lg:items-start` on the hero grid.
- **Test changes outside the plan:** `expectNoAxeViolations` in home.spec now waits for finite animations (a half-faded node reads as low contrast); `seo-coverage.test.ts` exempts the dev page; `e2e/support/servers.ts` lists `/it/_dev/diagrams`.
- **Playwright browser:** the dependency bump had moved Playwright to Chromium build 1254, which was not installed. Ran `pnpm exec playwright install chromium` (about 330 MB, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ms-playwright`).
- **Not measured:** Lighthouse (CI only; it will cover `/`, `/en`, and the other routes in `lighthouserc.json`, of which the home routes carry the diagram); Firefox/WebKit; real forced-colours rendering (the e2e only checks that stroke and fill are painted); screen-reader output of the figure; the contrast of the diagram strokes beyond the existing token-pair tests.
- **Limits:** the grid in the SVG (40 units) and the section grid (24 px) differ, so they do not align; the panel hides the section grid behind it. Legend hover is pointer only. Labels assume a monospace font about 0.6 em wide: a wider fallback font could crowd "03 DOCUMENTI". V2–V5 titles in ROADMAP are my provisional wording.
