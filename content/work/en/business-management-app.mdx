## What it shows

A back-office application for a fictional small business: sales, purchases, stock and cash flow, with dashboards, charts and realistic workflows. It shows how I design data-rich interfaces.

## How it is built

It runs entirely in the browser, with sample data generated locally: nothing is sent to a server, and a button resets everything so you can start over.

## Why it is a demo

It is an example of the kind of application I build, not a product to use: the data is made up and stays on your device.
