## What it shows

Three screens of a back-office for an invented neighbourhood shop, all working on the same data. At the till you ring up a sale and pay in cash or by card. The cash-flow dashboard shows the balance, the money in and out, and two charts, each with its figures available as a table. In purchase orders you reorder what is running low and move each order from draft to sent to received. Ring up a sale and the stock, the takings and the balance change together.

## Where the data lives

The sample shop is generated in the page from a fixed seed. Your changes are saved in this browser's own storage, so they are still there when you reload, and nowhere else: no account, no server, no cookie. A button erases them and restores the sample data.

## Stated limits

The shop, the products and every amount are invented. The rules are simplified on purpose: a single VAT rate, money counted on the day a sale is made or an order arrives, no invoices, no users, no sync between devices. It shows how I design data-heavy screens, not accounting software. Nothing is typed into it, so no personal data can enter.
