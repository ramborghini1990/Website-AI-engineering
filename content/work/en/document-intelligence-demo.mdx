## What it shows

The reading of sample documents included in the demo, such as fictional invoices and contracts: field extraction, classification and plain-language questions, with a pointer to the passage each answer comes from.

## Human review

Doubtful results are highlighted for review, as they would be in a real system: the person checks only what needs checking.

## Data

The demo uses only the sample documents provided with it. You cannot upload your own files, so nobody's data is processed.
