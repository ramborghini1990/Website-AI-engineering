## What it shows

Three sample documents with invented companies: an invoice, a service contract and a transport document. For each one the demo pulls out the key facts as structured fields, such as the invoice number, the amounts and the due date, and shows the words each value was read from. You can then pick a question, and the answer comes with the exact sentence, the page and the section it is based on. Where the document does not say, the answer says it cannot find it instead of guessing.

## Human review

Each document contains something a person should look at: a smudged order number, a handwritten change to a fee, a blurred weight. Those values are read but flagged, and they stay flagged until someone marks them as checked. Any answer that rests on one of them says so. The person checks only what needs checking.

## Stated limits

You cannot upload files: the demo opens only the three documents provided with it, so nobody's data is processed, and nothing is sent to a server. It does not use an AI model either: the fields are read and the answers composed with fixed rules, and the questions are a short fixed list, not free text. A real system would read your own documents with a language model, which needs an agreed purpose, a data-processing agreement and a retention rule first.
