## What it shows

A short list of invented companies and people. For each one the assistant writes a research summary, rates how well it fits the ideal customer and, only where contact makes sense, drafts a first message. It also says who should not be contacted, and why. The aim is to show how AI helps you prepare well, not how to send messages in bulk.

## Explicit human control

Every message goes through an approval step that cannot be skipped: a person reads it, edits it, confirms they read it and approves it, one prospect at a time. Only then does it show as scheduled. There is no "approve all" and no automatic follow-up.

## Stated limits

The demo does not connect to LinkedIn, reads no profile and automates no account: it uses only invented data, and the research and the rating are computed with fixed rules, not by an AI model. A real use would require the tools and APIs approved by the platform and respect for its terms: that is why I talk about assisted outreach and lead qualification, not about scraping or automation that works around limits.
