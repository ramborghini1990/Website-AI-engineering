## What it shows

A list of synthetic profiles, a short research summary for each and a draft of a first message. The aim is to show how AI helps you prepare well, not how to send messages in bulk.

## Explicit human control

Every message goes through a clearly visible approval step: a person reads it, edits it and decides whether to send it. The agent sends nothing on its own.

## Stated limits

The demo does not connect to LinkedIn and does not automate any account. A real use would require the tools and APIs approved by the platform and respect for its terms: that is why I talk about assisted outreach and lead qualification, not about automation that works around limits.
