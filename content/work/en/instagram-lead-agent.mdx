## What it shows

A simulation of the inbox and comments of a fictional business profile. The agent sorts each message (information request, sales interest, support, spam), scores the contacts and drafts a reply.

## Where a person steps in

Replies do not go out on their own: the draft is shown and approved by a person, and qualified contacts move on to the next step. The demo always shows what the agent decided and why.

## How it works in practice

An agent like this works through Instagram's official APIs for business accounts, within the platform's terms. The demo uses only synthetic profiles and messages: no real account is connected and no personal data is processed.
