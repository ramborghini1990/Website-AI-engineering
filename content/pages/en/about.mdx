## What I do

I work on four things: autonomous AI agents, process automation, production web applications and document intelligence. I combine them because real problems need them together: a useful agent needs reliable data, a clear interface and infrastructure that holds up. I work with startups, SMEs and enterprises in Italy and across the European Union.

## How I work

I work directly with you, with no middle layer: the person who listens to the problem is the one who designs and writes the code. I would rather start from a prototype evaluated on real cases than from a long specification, and I say right away when AI is not the right answer. The [way I work](/process) describes the phases one by one.

## What I care about

Quality you can verify, with automated tests and checks. Transparency about what the AI does and where a person steps in. Care for privacy and security from the design stage: collecting as little data as possible and keeping secrets away from the browser. Accessibility: what I build has to be usable by everyone.

## Where I am

I work from Turin, in Italian and in English. If you want to know whether I can help, [get in touch](/contact).
