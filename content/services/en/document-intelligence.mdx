## From piles of documents to usable data

Contracts, invoices, data sheets, forms: the information is there, but locked in files that are hard to search and compare. I build systems that read documents, extract the fields you need, classify them and let you search in plain language.

## Every result can be checked

Answers point to the passage of the document they come from. Extracted data is checked against defined rules and formats, and uncertain cases go to a person through a review interface. Before release I measure accuracy on a sample of your own documents, so you know what to expect.

## Where the data goes

Documents may contain personal or confidential data. Together we define where they are processed, what is kept and for how long, according to the type of data and the requirements that apply to your case. To start, [tell me](/contact) what documents you have and what you would like to get out of them.
