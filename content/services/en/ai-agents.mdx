## Where it works well

Agents pay off where work is made of many similar steps and decisions you can describe: qualifying a lead, routing a request to the right team, drafting a reply, finding and summarising information spread across several sources.

## How I build it

I start from one specific task and real examples. The agent gets only the tools and data it needs, with limited permissions. Before release I evaluate it on a set of test cases, and afterwards I track its quality and cost, because an agent needs the same watching as any other system.

## Controls and limits

At critical steps the agent prepares and a person approves, and every action is recorded. Integrations with platforms such as Instagram or LinkedIn go through their official APIs only, within their terms of use: I do not automate personal accounts or work around platform limits.
