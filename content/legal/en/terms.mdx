## 1. What the demos are

The demos in the Work section show how an agent, an automation or an application could work. They are **simulations**: they run in your browser with prepared scenarios and invented data, and they connect to no real account or service.

## 2. Artificial intelligence notice

Today no demo uses an artificial intelligence model in real time: the texts you see, even when they read as if an assistant wrote them, come from prepared scenarios. If a live mode with an AI model is ever switched on, before your first interaction I will tell you clearly that you are interacting with an artificial intelligence system (art. 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), I will mark generated content and I will explain which data would be sent and to whom. Live mode will be switched on only by you, with consent, and will be described on this page and in the [privacy notice](/privacy).

## 3. No reliance

The demos exist to illustrate an idea. The results, figures, texts, answers and citations you see are invented examples: they are not professional, legal, financial or tax advice, they are not real data, and you must not rely on them to make decisions. They are not a promise of results: a real project depends on your data, your systems and your goals, and is defined together with you before it starts.

## 4. Do not enter real data

Use only invented data. Do not type into the demos personal data of your own or of others, client data, trade secrets or credentials. In simulation mode what you type stays in your browser; the business-suite demo keeps your changes in the browser's database until you reset it or clear the site's data (see the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy)).

## 5. People, companies and platforms

Names, profiles, messages, documents and companies in the demos are invented. Any resemblance to real people or companies is coincidental. Demos that echo the look of platforms such as Instagram or LinkedIn are not connected to them, use no real accounts and publish or send nothing: the names and trademarks belong to their owners (see the [legal notice](/legal)).

## 6. Fair use

You may use the demos to try and evaluate them. Do not use them to attack, overload or get around the site, or to extract its content automatically. I may limit or suspend access when needed to protect the site.

## 7. Availability and liability

The demos are provided "as is", with no guarantee of availability or of being free of errors, and they may change or be removed. To the extent the law allows, I am not liable for damage arising from using the demos or relying on their content. This does not exclude liability that the law does not allow to be excluded, nor consumers' mandatory rights.

When these terms change, the version and date at the top of the page change. Italian law applies, without prejudice to the mandatory consumer protection rules of the country where you live.
