## 1. In short

This site uses no profiling cookies, no advertising cookies and no analytics tools that recognise you. It saves in your browser only what it needs to work and to remember your choices. One third-party item, the calendar for booking a call, loads only if you allow it.

The tables below are printed from the single list the site uses for itself: if the site started saving one more item, or using a new provider, the project's tests would fail until this page lists it.

## 3. Third-party content you can allow

## 4. How you choose and how you change your mind

- On your first visit the banner asks for your choice. "Reject all" and "Accept all" look the same. Closing the banner with the X counts as rejecting.
- Scrolling or carrying on browsing is not consent, and no box is pre-ticked.
- On any page, at the bottom, "Cookie preferences" reopens your choices: you can change them or withdraw consent as easily as you gave it. If you withdraw, the embedded content is removed from the page.
- If you reject, I do not ask again for at least six months. I ask sooner if the banner's text or categories change materially, or if your choice can no longer be read (for example you cleared your cookies).
- Cookies a provider sets inside its own content cannot be deleted from this site: you can do that in your browser settings.

## 5. The receipt of your choice

To be able to prove that you chose, I keep on the server a receipt with a random code, the date and time, the version of the banner text, the method (accept all, reject all, customise, close) and your choice about the calendar. It holds no IP address, browser or account. The code also appears in the "consent" cookie and in the preferences, so you can quote it in a request. For details see the [privacy notice](/privacy).

## 7. Statistics

The site uses no third-party analytics. The demos only count, per day, how many times they are started and completed, with no cookie, no identifier and no IP address: nobody can be recognised.

## 8. Browser settings

You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings: some features, such as remembering the theme, the banner or your changes in the business-suite demo, will stop working. For more about cookies in general you can visit the website of the [Italian data protection authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
