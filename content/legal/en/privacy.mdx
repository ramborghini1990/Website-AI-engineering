## 2. Which data I process, why and for how long

Each purpose has its own legal basis and retention period. I process no data beyond what is listed and do not use data for purposes other than those stated.

- **Answering your request.** Data: name, email, company (optional), type of need, description, budget band, timeline and the language of the page. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Providing the data is necessary to get an answer; without it the form cannot be sent. Retention: 24 months from the last contact, unless a contract follows: in that case the data needed follow the tax and accounting retention duties.
- **News and articles, only if you choose.** The box in the form is never pre-ticked and is not needed to get an answer. Data: your choice, the version of the text you saw and the time. Legal basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it, without affecting processing already done. Retention: until you withdraw.
- **Security and protection of the form against abuse.** The form has a field that is invisible to people and a security check (Cloudflare Turnstile). To limit repeated requests I keep, for a few minutes, a code derived from your IP address with a secret key, not the IP address itself. Legal basis: legitimate interest in protecting the site (art. 6(1)(f)). You have the right to object (see below).
- **Proving your cookie choice.** When you choose in the banner I keep a receipt with a random code, the date and time, the version of the banner text, the method of the choice and whether you allowed the booking calendar. The receipt holds no IP address, browser or account. Legal basis: the duty to be able to demonstrate consent (art. 7(1)) and legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)). Retention: for as long as it may be needed to prove the choice; the period will be set and stated here before the site is finally published.
- **Technical operation of the site.** The host of the site records the IP address, browser, page requested and time in technical logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running and protecting the site (art. 6(1)(f)). The site writes no form content, emails, phone numbers, names or IP addresses into its own logs.
- **Booking a call.** The Cal.com calendar loads only if you allow it. Data: what you enter on the booking page, handled by Cal.com under its own notice. Legal basis: your consent to the loading (art. 6(1)(a)).
- **Demo statistics.** The site counts, per day, how many times a demo is started and completed. The counts hold no identifier, IP address, browser, page or language and cannot be used to recognise anyone: they are not personal data.

## 4. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Where a provider allows it I have chosen European Union regions. Some providers are US companies: when data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, as stated in the agreement with the provider. If you want a copy or more information about the safeguards, write to me.

## 5. No automated decisions

I take no decisions based solely on automated processing that affect you, and I do no profiling. The site's demos are simulations with invented data: see the [terms for the demos](/terms).

## 6. Cookies and similar tools

The site uses only technical tools and, with your consent, the third-party calendar. The full list and how to change your choice are in the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy).

## 7. Your rights

At any time you can ask me for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and, for processing based on legitimate interest, you can object (arts. 15-22 GDPR). Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise your rights use the [contact form](/contact). To avoid handing data to the wrong person I will reply to the address you wrote from. I answer within one month of the request (art. 12(3) GDPR), extendable in complex cases, with notice.

If you believe the processing breaches the rules you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante ([www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)), or with the authority of the country where you live or work.
